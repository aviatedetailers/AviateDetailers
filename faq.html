Questions

Frequently asked questions.

Everything you need to know before you book. Don't see your question? Call or text us.

Do I need to be there?

No. As long as we can get to your aircraft, you don't need to be there. Add the hangar number, tie-down spot or any access instructions in the notes when you book.

When we're done, we text or email you a link with before and after photos of your aircraft.

Do you need water or power from the hangar?

No, we bring everything. We use a rinseless wash, so there's no hose and no water hookup, and our tools are cordless.

Does my aircraft need to be in a hangar?

No. We can detail it in a hangar or on a tie-down, whichever is easier for you.

How long does a detail take?

A Full detail on a small single, like a Cessna 172, usually takes about 2 to 3 hours. Larger aircraft and add-ons like wax take longer.

What happens if it rains?

If your aircraft is in a hangar, we can usually still do the job. If it's outside, we'll reschedule with you.

How much does it cost?

You can see prices right on our quote form for most single-engine aircraft and turboprops. Pick your aircraft and services and the total adds up as you go.

Twins, jets, helicopters and specialty aircraft get a custom quote. We confirm the final price when we reply to your request.

What's your cancellation policy?

Free cancellation up to 3 hours before your start time. Later cancellations are charged 25% of the job price.

Can you come late at night?

Yes. You can pick a start time late at night on the booking calendar. Starts from 11 PM to 4 AM have an after-hours fee, which is shown when you pick the time.

Do you offer recurring plans?

Yes. Tick "Interested in a recurring plan?" on the quote form and pick how often, and we'll reach out with plan pricing. Regular visits keep bugs, grime and exhaust stains from building up.

Which airports do you serve?

We work at general-aviation airports across New Jersey, including Lakewood (N12), Monmouth Executive (KBLM) and Ocean County (KMJX). At another airport? Ask us.

Are you insured?

Yes, Aviate Detailers is fully insured.

What products do you use?

Aviation-grade products chosen for aircraft paint, plexiglass and interiors, including cleaners that meet Boeing's aircraft cleaning specifications. More on our What we use page.

What about scratches or chips that are already there?

Before we start, we walk around your aircraft and photograph any existing scratches, dents or chips, so there's a clear record of its condition before we touch it.
